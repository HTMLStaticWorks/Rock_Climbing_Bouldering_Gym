Smart Boards

Precision Training Boards

Bluetooth-enabled training holds linked to international route directories.

Featuring specific wood rungs (15mm, 20mm, 32mm) at exact angles. Designed to optimize dynamic muscle pull and fingertip contact power.

The Setting Cycle

Our routesetting crew resets two full zones every week. Experience fresh holds, unique body positioning sequences, and creative problems designed to benchmark your physical limit.

Science Room

Biomechanical Climb Lab

Diagnose plates, evaluate fingertip contact forces, and optimize center-of-gravity vectors. We bring high-end sports science labs straight to local Denver athletes.

Force Dynamo Gauges

Kinematic Camera Tracking

Slow-motion high frame rate capture to evaluate drag, dynamic shifts, and joint stabilization.
