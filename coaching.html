Our Training Roadmap

A structured four-phase system designed to ensure safe, continuous progress.

01

Initial Assessment

Evaluate mobility limits, finger force output, and core tension dynamics on different board shapes.

02

Customized Target Log

Receive weekly hangboard weights, rest timing cycles, and core conditioning routines.

03

Video Beta Review

Review climbing movements frame-by-frame to identify energy leaks and center of mass errors.

04

Redpoint Checkpoint

Attempt goal routes with in-session coach support to verify technical mastery and claim badges.
