Beginner FAQs

Do I need special shoes?

We provide specialized, high-grip climbing rental shoes during all introductory classes.

What if I am afraid of heights?

Our bouldering zones are only 4.5 meters tall with soft foam padding, so you can climb without ever using ropes.
