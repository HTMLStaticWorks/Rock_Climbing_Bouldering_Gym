Friendly Competition

Bouldering Team Leagues

Form a crew of four climbers and compete in our 8-week seasonal leagues. Climbers of all skill levels are grouped dynamically, allowing V3 weekend climbers to score points alongside V10 comp athletes. Track your weekly team progress live on the member dashboard.

  • Handicap grade systems to balance rookie and veteran scores.
  • Weekly theme nights, raffle entries, and food truck socials.
  • Seasonal trophies and gear prizes from Black Diamond & La Sportiva.

Stewardship

Crag Preservation Projects

We believe in protecting the real-world rock systems that inspire our indoor routes. SummitX partners with the Colorado Climbing Coalition to organize monthly trail maintenance, chalk removal, and bolt safety inspections at popular local bouldering sectors.

Trail Restoration

Building stone steps and clearing erosion paths to prevent damage to local vegetation.

Hardware Inspections

Replacing worn anchor setups and checking standard expansion bolts on popular routes.
